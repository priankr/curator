## Andrej Karpathy: Software Is Changing (Again)
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCEmiRjPEtQ
tags: strategy

Karpathy's AI Startup School keynote on Software 3.0, where you program in English, and why LLMs work like a new kind of operating system. Covers designing apps with partial autonomy and keeping people in the loop.

## Christian Idiodi: The Essence of Product Management
url: https://youtu.be/SXYc5RoU3Lg
tags: product-management

The SVPG partner on what product managers actually do, built around four risks: value, usability, viability and feasibility. Also covers reference customers and coaching. From Lenny's Podcast.

## Jiaona Zhang: Building a Company OS in Claude Code
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qsDX0PMKcaE
tags: claude-code, teams

Laurel's CPO screenshares her team's full Company OS, including the agent pipeline and how non-technical teammates ship to production. Hosted by Aakash Gupta.

## Ankur Goyal: Evals Are the New PRD
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=71qvIkO9d_A
tags: evals

The Braintrust founder explains why every PM needs evals, then builds one from scratch using a data, task and scores framework. Hosted by Aakash Gupta.

## Teresa Torres: A Personal OS in Claude Code
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBJdwRPO1QE
tags: claude-code, productivity

The product coach shows how she runs her work and life from Claude Code and a notes app, from a daily to-do command to delegated research and writing. Hosted by Peter Yang.

## Aman Khan: A Beginner's Course on AI Evals
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TL527yTpxlk
tags: evals

Two PMs build evals for an AI customer support agent live, from labelling a golden dataset to aligning LLM judges. A complete introduction if evals are new to you. Hosted by Peter Yang.

## Elizabeth Stone: Systems Thinkers in the AI Era
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t0GiTyz4syY
tags: leadership, teams

Netflix's CPTO on why systems thinking is now the skill she hires for most, and how to manage the flood of AI-generated work without losing quality. From Lenny's Podcast.

## Shreyas Doshi: The Art of Product Management
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YP_QghPLG-8
tags: product-management, leadership

The former Stripe product leader on product, strategy, psychology and leadership, full of practical frameworks. From Lenny's Podcast.

## Hilary Gridley: Automating Your Life with Claude Code
url: https://youtu.be/LJ1YZ3Uek3g
tags: claude-code, productivity

Her "anti-system system" for personal productivity: keep the setup simple and let the AI learn your preferences as you go. From How I AI.

## Ryan Carson: A 3-Step AI Coding Workflow
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fD4ktSkNCw4
tags: ai-coding

The five-time founder turns vibe coding into a structured process with a three-file system, AI-written PRDs and task lists, and a step-by-step build in Cursor. From How I AI.

## Felix Rieseberg: How the Claude Cowork Engineer Uses Claude
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-tdNsYi8AXs
tags: claude-code, productivity

The engineering lead for Claude Cowork shows how he uses Claude on real problems, from turning a floor plan into a 3D walkthrough to a $20 button that approves Claude's actions. His rule: go one layer of abstraction up and let Claude find the data itself. From How I AI.

## Hiten Shah: Advanced ChatGPT for Work
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwtEajzgjWY
tags: productivity

The serial founder turns ChatGPT into a workplace coach and sales strategist, including an AI version of your boss built from their operating manual. The lesson throughout: load the right context and show it what great looks like. From How I AI.

## Felix Lee: Design and Code with Claude Code and Figma
url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ydiMKfljb-I
tags: claude-code, design

Builds apps that match their Figma designs with Claude Code and the Figma MCP server, turns a FigJam flowchart into a working game and converts code back into Figma components. Hosted by Peter Yang.
