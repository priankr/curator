title: Visual References
short: Visuals

---

## NASA Image of the Day
url: https://www.nasa.gov/image-of-the-day/
tags: space, photography

Interesting space images. Most are not copyrighted, but check the credit line, because some images come from partners.

## The Public Domain Review
url: https://publicdomainreview.org/collections/images/
tags: public-domain, history

Curated sets of public domain images, each with an essay on where they came from. Slower to browse than a database, but every find comes with its story.

## The British Library
url: https://www.flickr.com/photos/britishlibrary/albums
tags: public-domain, books, illustration, history
image: none

Over a million images from 19th century books. The albums group them by theme, such as maps, decorative letters and portraits. Great for older, more obscure visuals.

## Cooper Hewitt Collection
url: https://www.si.edu/collections/cooper-hewitt
tags: open-access, museum, design
image: none

The Smithsonian's design museum: posters, wallpaper, textiles, drawings and product design. Much of it is in Smithsonian Open Access, so many images are free to reuse.

## Internet Archive Book Images
url: https://www.flickr.com/photos/internetarchivebookimages/
tags: public-domain, books, illustration, history
image: none

Millions of illustrations cut from scanned books.

## The Cleveland Museum of Art
url: https://www.clevelandart.org/art/collection/search
tags: open-access, museum, art
image: none

Tens of thousands of artworks with free, high-resolution downloads.

## National Gallery of Art
url: https://www.nga.gov/artwork-search?download=1
tags: open-access, museum, art
image: none

American and European painting, prints and drawings.

## Art Institute of Chicago
url: https://www.artic.edu/collection
tags: open-access, museum, art
image: none

A large collection of artworks, with public domain works free to download at high resolution.

## The Metropolitan Museum
url: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search
tags: open-access, museum, art
image: none

Hundreds of thousands of public domain images across 5,000 years of art, from armour to fashion.

## Readymag Design Almanac
url: https://readymag.com/almanac/typography/
tags: typography
image: none

A great resource for identifying font combinations and typeface pairings.
